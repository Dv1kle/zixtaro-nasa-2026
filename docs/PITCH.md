# Zixtaro: entry pitch

NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026, "Field Shift: Adapting Farms with NASA Data". About 1 minute spoken.

---

Hi, we're team Zixtaro.

Every spring a farmer decides what goes into the field next. That choice used to rest on experience - how the rain
fell, when the frosts came. But the climate that experience was built on is changing.

We're going to build a tool that helps with that choice.

You pick your field anywhere on Earth. We pull decades of NASA data for that exact spot - temperature and rain from
NASA POWER and GPM, soil moisture from SMAP, crop health from MODIS, groundwater from GRACE, terrain from SRTM - and
add the soil underneath it.

From that, the tool will rank the crops this field can grow next, and compare the rotation the farm uses today with
at least three alternatives. For each one it will explain why it's suggested, what the data says, what it does for
the soil, water and income, and what's still uncertain and needs a local expert's eye.

The farmer sets the priorities - protect the soil, save water, or earn more - and the plan adjusts. They can also see
how each rotation holds up in a warmer climate.

It will run in any browser, with no installation, and every number on the screen will show where it came from.

We're not replacing the farmer's judgment. We want to give it NASA's view from orbit.
